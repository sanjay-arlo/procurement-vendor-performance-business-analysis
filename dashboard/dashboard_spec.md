# Power BI Dashboard Specification

## Executive Overview
KPI cards: Total Spend, On-Time %, Defect Rate %, Avg Lead Time, Estimated Quality Cost.

## Visuals
1. Spend by Vendor
2. On-Time Delivery vs Defect Rate scatter
3. Vendor scorecard matrix
4. Monthly procurement spend trend
5. Spend concentration / Pareto
6. Vendor detail drill-through

## Filters
Vendor | Category | Month | Business Unit

## Management Story
Identify high-spend vendors with weak service or quality and prioritise renegotiation, supplier development, or replacement.
