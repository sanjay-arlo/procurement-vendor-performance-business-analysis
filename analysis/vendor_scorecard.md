# Vendor Scorecard Framework

Evaluate suppliers using a balanced scorecard rather than purchase price alone.

## Core KPIs
- Total Spend
- On-Time Delivery %
- Average Lead Time
- Defect Rate %
- Unit Price Variance %
- Purchase Order Fill Rate %
- Estimated Cost of Quality
- Estimated Delay Cost

## Decision Categories
- Strategic: high spend and high business importance
- Preferred: strong performance with reliable service
- Watchlist: repeated KPI deterioration
- Renegotiate: attractive price but weak service/quality
- Replace/Develop: persistent performance problems

## Business Principle
A supplier with a low quoted price can still create a higher total cost when defects, delays, expediting, and operational disruption are included.
