# Procurement & Vendor Performance Business Analysis

Business Analyst case study focused on supplier performance, procurement spend, service levels, quality, cost drivers, and vendor-management decisions.

## Business Objective

Identify unreliable or costly suppliers, understand the drivers of procurement inefficiency, and support vendor negotiation and sourcing decisions.

## Key Questions
- Which vendors receive the largest share of spend?
- Which suppliers miss delivery SLAs most often?
- Where are quality defects concentrated?
- Are low-price suppliers still economical after delays and defects?
- Which vendors should be retained, renegotiated, developed, or replaced?

## Deliverables
- Synthetic procurement dataset
- SQL business analysis
- Python/Pandas analysis
- Vendor KPI scorecard
- Power BI dashboard specification
- Management recommendations

## Tech Stack
Excel | SQL/MySQL | Python/Pandas | Power BI | DAX

## Data Note
This is a synthetic portfolio case study created for business-analysis demonstration. Supplier and financial figures are illustrative.
